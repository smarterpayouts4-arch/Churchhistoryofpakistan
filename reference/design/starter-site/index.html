Preserving History • Honouring Our Forefathers • Inspiring Future Generations

History: Church of Pakistan is a platform dedicated to informing the present and coming generations about the contributions, sacrifices, and service of our forefathers who helped shape the life and witness of the Church and its people in Pakistan.

Our purpose is to preserve historical memories and make them accessible through photographs, published documents, family records, and personal memories.

Selected documented milestones

A Journey Through Time

  1. 1852

    Sialkot

    First stone laid for Holy Trinity Cathedral Church.

  2. 1887

    Lahore

    Cathedral Church of the Resurrection constructed and consecrated.

  3. 1970

    Church Union

    Church of Pakistan formed through the union of Anglican, Methodist, Lutheran and Scottish Presbyterian traditions.

  4. 1980

    Eight Dioceses

    Hyderabad, Raiwind, Faisalabad and Peshawar added to the four original dioceses.

  5. 2011

    Raiwind

    New Central Cathedral of Praying Hands dedicated at Warris Road, Lahore.

A personal commitment

My Connection to the Church

I am a Christian and a born member of the Church of Pakistan.

I served as a Member of the Executive Committee of the Church of Pakistan from 1991 to 2017.

My father, Rt. Rev. Bishop Johnston Safdar Qadir Bakhsh, served as a Bishop and Moderator of the Church of Pakistan and devoted his life to the service of the Church.

This page is therefore not only a historical project but also a personal commitment to preserve the heritage and living memories of the Church of Pakistan for generations to come.

Our Guiding Principle

“Preserve History for the Coming Generation.”

We believe that history belongs to everyone. Our aim is to preserve it faithfully, respectfully, and responsibly—so that future generations can learn from the people, events, sacrifices, and achievements that shaped the Church of Pakistan.
