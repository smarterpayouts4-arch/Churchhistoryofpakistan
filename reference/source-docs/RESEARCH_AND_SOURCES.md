# Research & Source Register

Research performed September 17, 2026 for the website build package.

## Church of Pakistan — institutional history

The official Church of Pakistan states that it came into existence in **1970** through the union of four traditions: Anglican, Methodist, Lutheran, and Presbyterian (Scottish). It says the church initially had the dioceses of Karachi, Multan, Lahore, and Sialkot; in **1980**, Hyderabad, Raiwind, Faisalabad, and Peshawar were created, making eight active dioceses.

Sources:
- Church of Pakistan — About Us: https://www.cop.org.pk/About-Us/
- Church of Pakistan — Finance / list of eight dioceses: https://www.cop.org.pk/Finance/
- National Council of Churches in Pakistan — Member Churches: https://nccpakistan.org.pk/member-churches/

- **Central Cathedral of Praying Hands**, Lahore
- The Diocese of Raiwind newsletter names the Central Cathedral of Praying Hands, Lahore.
- A Church of Scotland World Mission post records the dedication of the new cathedral at Warris Road, Lahore on **9 October 2011**, on the site of the former Central Church.
- Sources:

### Sialkot
- **Holy Trinity Cathedral Church / Sialkot Cathedral**
- The Mall / Quaid-i-Azam Road, Sialkot Cantonment
- Punjab government district page says the first stone was laid **1 March 1852** and the church was consecrated **30 January 1857**.
- Sources:
  - https://sialkot.punjab.gov.pk/important_places

## Editorial caution

Historical material can contain variant spellings, dates, titles, and denominational terminology. On each published record:
1. preserve the wording of primary documents in transcription;
2. distinguish source text from editorial interpretation;
3. record uncertainty;
4. avoid silently “correcting” family memories into factual claims;
5. maintain a citation trail.
