                             Javed Qadir Bakhsh
               A Life of Public Service, Christian Leadership and Preservation of History


Introduction
Javed Qadir Bakhsh is a Pakistani public servant, Christian leader, Formanite, and historian
whose life has been shaped by three enduring commitments: service to the nation, service to
the Church, and preservation of history for future generations.
                                     His journey encompasses Christian student leadership, a
                                     distinguished career in the Government of Pakistan,
                                     international professional training, and decades of active
                                     participation in the life and administration of the Church of
                                     Pakistan.
                                     In 1962, following his father’s transfer from Christ Church,
                                     Drigh Road, Karachi, to the Central Cathedral of Praying
                                     Hands, Lahore, a new chapter began in his life. He moved to
                                     Lahore with his family and was admitted to Cathedral
                                     School, Hall Road. It was here that his formative years of
                                     education and character development took shape. He
                                     completed his Matriculation in 1969, laying the foundation
                                     for the remarkable journey of faith, service and leadership
that would follow.


Forman Christian College — The Beginning of Leadership

Javed Qadir Bakhsh is a proud Formanite,
having graduated from Forman Christian
College, Lahore, in 1974, and completed his
postgraduate studies in 1976.
During his student years, he was actively
involved in the Student Christian Movement
(SCM) and served as:
• General       Secretary,    Student    Christian
Movement
• Student Secretary,          National    Student
Christian Movement
His student leadership provided an important foundation for his later service to the Church
and to the wider community.
Public Service to Pakistan
After completing his education, Javed Qadir Bakhsh entered the
Government of Pakistan and pursued a career devoted to public
service.
He ultimately served as Joint Census Commissioner, Government of
Pakistan, contributing to the country's national census and
demographic administration.
His professional responsibilities required a high degree of accuracy,
organisation and understanding of population data—information
essential for national planning and development.


Bush Scholarship — International Professional Training
A particularly significant milestone in his public-service career came in 1989, when Javed Qadir
Bakhsh earned and qualified for a scholarship offered by the Government of Pakistan under
the Bush Scholarship programme.
Under this scholarship, he travelled to the United States of America for professional exposure
and training at the U.S. Census Bureau in Washington, D.C.
His association with the U.S. Census Bureau provided him
with an opportunity to observe and learn from one of the
world's leading institutions in population census
administration, demographic statistics and census
methodology.
The experience broadened his professional knowledge and
brought an important international dimension to his work
with the Government of Pakistan.
His selection for the scholarship was also a recognition of his professional capabilities and
contribution to Pakistan's census administration.
He was in charge of Population Census organization, Punjab province. It is biggest province of
Pakistan.
Beside this he headed Coordination Department in Islamabad.
He has vast experience of field operations of biggest data collection and analysing agency in
Pakistan.

A Passion for Church History
One of the defining features of Javed Qadir Bakhsh's continuing work has been his passion for
preserving the history of the Church of Pakistan and the Christian community in Pakistan.
He has devoted considerable attention to documenting the formation and development of
the Church, its dioceses, bishops, clergy, lay leaders and institutions.
He believes that history is not merely a record of dates and events. It is the collective memory
of a community.
His particular concern is that the contributions of clergy, lay people, students and Christian
institutions should be properly recorded so that they are not lost with the passing of
generations.
   •   Preserving the Role of the Laity
   Javed Qadir Bakhsh has also shown a strong interest in documenting and strengthening
   the role of the laity in the Church.
   His own experience of Christian student leadership and later participation in Church
   committees has given him an appreciation of the importance of meaningful cooperation
   between clergy and lay members.
   He believes that responsible participation by lay people strengthens the Church and
   contributes to transparent, effective and representative Church administration.
   •   Preserving the Legacy of Bishop Johnston Safdar Qadir Bakhsh
   Another important part of his historical work is the preservation
   of the life and legacy of his father, Bishop Johnston Safdar Qadir
   Bakhsh, a pioneering leader of the Church of Pakistan.
   Through biographical research, historical documentation and the
   collection of memories and records, Javed seeks to preserve his
   father's contribution for future generations.
   This work is both a family tribute and part of a wider effort to
   preserve the history of the early Church of Pakistan and the
   generation of leaders who helped shape it.
   •   A Bridge Between Generations
   Javed Qadir Bakhsh's life represents a bridge between generations.
   From his student leadership at Forman Christian College, through his professional career
   in the Government of Pakistan and his international experience at the U.S. Census Bureau,
   to his continuing service in the Church of Pakistan, his journey has remained rooted in
   leadership, service and responsibility.
   He believes that younger generations should have access to the stories, documents,
   photographs and experiences of those who came before them.
   His guiding principle is:
   “Preserve history for the coming generations.”


A Life Defined by Service
Four major themes characterise his journey:
   ➢ Education — Forman Christian College and postgraduate studies.
   ➢ Student Leadership — General Secretary of the Student Christian Movement and
     Student Secretary of the National SCM.
   ➢ Public Service — A distinguished career in the Government of Pakistan culminating in
     service as Joint Census Commissioner, including international professional exposure at
     the U.S. Census Bureau through the Bush Scholarship in 1989.
   ➢ Christian and Historical Service — Continuing involvement in the Church of Pakistan
     through the Executive Committee, Parish Committee, Clergy Ordination Committee
     and Education Board, together with his work to preserve Christian history.
Legacy and Continuing Mission
Javed Qadir Bakhsh's continuing mission is to ensure that the history of Christian institutions
and communities in Pakistan is recorded, preserved and made available to future generations.
His work seeks to bring together:
People • Places • Institutions • Documents • Photographs • Memories • Church Records •
Personal Testimonies
so that an important part of Pakistan's Christian heritage is not forgotten.




Javed Qadir Bakhsh has created a Facebook page, “History: Church of Pakistan,” through
which he shares photographs and memories of clergy, lay leaders, and other people who have
served the Church of Pakistan. His aim is to preserve the contributions of church leaders and
keep the history of the Church alive for present and future generations.
He also shares facts, figures, and historical information from the official minutes and records
of the Church, making this valuable material accessible to a wider audience and helping
people understand and remember the Church’s journey.
In addition, he has initiated two programmes on Facebook:
Rab Ki Khidmat (Service of the Spirit) — focusing on faith, Christian service, and spiritual
encouragement.
Motivational Programmes for Youngsters — designed to encourage and inspire young people
in their personal, professional, and spiritual journeys.
As part of the motivational programme, he would like to interview young professionals and
successful young Pakistanis, giving them an opportunity to share their experiences,
challenges, achievements, and lessons learned. The purpose is to inspire and motivate the
younger generation and show them positive examples of young people making a meaningful
contribution to society.
His vision is to preserve the past, celebrate those who served, and inspire the younger
generation to build a better future.
Conclusion
The life of Javed Qadir Bakhsh is a story of continuing service—from student Christian
leadership to national public service, from international professional experience to Church
administration, and from personal family history to the preservation of a wider Christian
heritage.
His journey reflects a simple conviction:
“Service is a responsibility, and history is a legacy.”
He believes that we preserve history not simply to remember the past, but to give future
generations the knowledge and wisdom they need to understand their heritage and build
upon it.
