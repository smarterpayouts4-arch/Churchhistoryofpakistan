## Crop-risk method

For each native asset, the board compares native aspect to its public card aspect (4:3 archive/People/church, 9:16 interview, 3:4 hero). It recommends `contain` when the absolute aspect delta is greater than 0.25 or orientation mismatches. Risk is **high** for an orientation mismatch or delta greater than 0.4, **medium** for delta greater than 0.25, otherwise **low**.

All high/medium items below now render with `contain` in the affected public archive, People, church, or Reel frames, even where the algorithm would otherwise consider a closer-ratio asset low risk.
